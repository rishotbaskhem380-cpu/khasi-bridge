Talkweave — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Talkweave ("the app") is a voice and text translation app. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

What the app collects

  • Text you choose to translate — sent securely to our backend, which forwards it to Google's Translation API to get a translation back. This text is not stored permanently; it is only used to produce your translation.
  • A random device ID — generated on your device and stored locally. It contains no personal information and is used only to apply fair usage limits so the free service can't be abused.
  • Premium email (optional) — if you choose to enter the email you used to pay via Ko-fi, it is used only to check whether your account has premium access. It is not used for marketing and is not shared with anyone else.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not require account creation or login.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, or files.
  • It does not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Microphone access

The app uses your device's speech recognition (built into your browser/Android system) to convert your speech to text for translation. Audio is processed by your device/browser's own speech recognition service, not stored or sent to us directly.

Third-party services used

  • Google Cloud Translation API — translates the text you submit. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Ko-fi — used only if you choose to support the project or subscribe to premium. See Ko-fi's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Translated text is not stored after your translation is returned. Usage counters (for rate limiting) are automatically deleted after a short period (a few days to about a month).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Ko-fi page linked in the app.
